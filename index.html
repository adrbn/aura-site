OfflineDownload anything. What you stream caches itself.

Instant Mix

Start a radio from any song or artist. When the queue runs dry, autoplay keeps going. Save any mix as a real playlist on your server.

Made For You

Time-of-day, mood and genre mixes built from your own library, with covers drawn for each one. Plus a Year Wrapped of your listening.

  • SiriPlay, pause, skip, shuffle, repeat, favourite. “Play” a playlist or an album by name.
  • Search that forgivesFuzzy, ranked by relevance, and it remembers what you actually played.
  • Several serversKeep more than one profile and switch from the Home title.
  • Lock Screen & IslandControls on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and in the Dynamic Island.

Every word,as it’s sung.

Word-by-word highlight when your server has the timing, line by line when it doesn’t, LRCLIB for the rest. The lines around the one being sung step back in size, blur and brightness.

Scroll to sing along. Tap a line to jump to it, as you would in the app.

Your music stays yours.

Aura talks to the server you set up. There’s no account to create, and nothing is sent to the developer.

No account. Nothing to sign up for.

Add your server address, a username and a password, and you’re listening.

Keychain. Passwords stay encrypted.

Server passwords live in the iOS Keychain, never in plain text.

No tracking. Nothing comes back to us.

No analytics, no developer server. Lyrics, credits and share links use public services, each one listed in the privacy policy.

Aura is a client, not a server.

It plays the library you host on Navidrome or any Subsonic server. It doesn’t host or provide any music.

Get Aura on your iPhone

Aura runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Pick the way that suits you.

  1. Sideload the IPA

    Download Aura.ipa from GitHub Releases, then open it in AltStore or SideStore on your iPhone.

  2. Build it yourself

    Open the project in Xcode 26 and run the “Aura” scheme on your device.

    git clone https://github.com/adrbn/aura
  3. App Store

    Coming soon. Installed and updated the usual way.
