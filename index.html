Sideload build only

Download whatthe Radar finds.

The sideload build works with slskd, a Soulseek client you run next to your server. When a release or a song isn’t in your library, Get It downloads it and your server adds it.

  • Get It is on Radar releases, on Deezer results in Search, and on the heart while a preview plays in Now Playing. On a release’s page you can pick single songs.
  • Aura chooses the copy with the most of the release’s tracks, in the best quality available, from a peer that can start now. If a download stalls, it tries another.
  • Progress shows in a card above the mini player and in a Live Activity. The time estimate comes from how long your server took to import the last few releases.
  • If you’re still listening to the preview when the song arrives, playback switches to the full track at the same point.

Needs your own slskd server. Not in the App Store version. Only download music you have the right to.

And all the rest.

  • Siri, in two languagesPlay, pause, skip, shuffle, repeat, favourite, or a playlist or album by name, with the app closed. “Play my favourites in Aura”, or « Joue mes favoris sur Aura ».
  • In the carCarPlay’s Now Playing, and shuffle and repeat buttons that work from the car stereo or a remote.
  • Lock Screen & IslandControls on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and in the Dynamic Island, with a Live Activity and AirPlay.
  • Search that forgivesFuzzy, ranked so what you typed comes first, and it remembers what you played. With the Radar on, it also finds what your server doesn’t have yet.
  • Playlists, filteredA grid of covers or a list, narrowed in a tap to the pinned ones, radios, mixes, yours, shared or downloaded.
  • A real queuePlay next, add to the end, drag to reorder. And a sleep timer that fades out rather than cutting.
  • Pages in colourAlbums, playlists, mixes and radios open in the colour of their artwork, and the tabs glow with what’s playing.
  • No blank coversWhen your server has no artwork for an album, Aura finds the real cover in Deezer’s catalogue.
  • Your lookLight or dark, nine accent colours, tabs in the order you like, and the list density you want.
  • Nightstand clockSwitch it on, then turn the phone sideways at night: album art and the time.
  • Several serversKeep more than one profile and switch from the Home title.
  • Share linksSend a song as links that open it on other services, found through Apple’s and Deezer’s public search.

Every word,as it’s sung.

Word by word when your server has the timing, line by line when it doesn’t, LRCLIB for the rest. For a duet or a remix, Aura checks the words match the version playing, and Radar previews show their lyrics too. Add your own free Gemini key, and a tap puts a translation in your language under each line.

Scroll to sing along, or tap a line to jump to it.

Your music stays yours.

Aura talks to the server you set up. There’s no account to create, and nothing is sent to the developer.

No account. Nothing to sign up for.

Add your server address, a username and a password, and you’re listening.

Keychain. Passwords stay encrypted.

Server passwords live in the iOS Keychain, never in plain text.

No tracking. Nothing comes back to us.

No analytics, no developer server. Lyrics, credits, artist photos, the Radar and share links use public services, each one listed in the privacy policy.

Aura is a client, not a server.

It plays the library you host on Navidrome or any Subsonic server. It doesn’t host or provide any music.

Get Aura on your iPhone

Aura runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Pick the way that suits you.

  1. Sideload the IPA

    Download Aura.ipa from GitHub Releases, then open it in AltStore or SideStore on your iPhone. This build adds Get It, if you run slskd.

  2. Build it yourself

    Open the project in Xcode 26 and run the “Aura” scheme on your device.

    git clone https://github.com/adrbn/aura
  3. App Store

    Coming soon. Everything but Get It, installed and updated the usual way.
